This is the template for a Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement ("Corporate CLA"). The Project Owner accepts contributions from corporations and other legal entities under this template. This template is not legal advice. Independent legal advice is recommended before relying on it in a dispute.

The individual Contributor Licence Agreement is [CLA.md](CLA.md). Read it alongside this template: the licences and obligations are the same, applied at entity level.

## 1. Purpose

This Corporate CLA clarifies the intellectual property licence granted by the Corporation to the Project Owner for Contributions made to Runesgalder by its Designated Contributors. The Corporation retains ownership of its Contributions. This Corporate CLA does not transfer copyright ownership to the Project Owner.

This project uses a CLA, not a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO). This template is aligned with [CLA.md](CLA.md) version 1.0 and is inspired by the Apache Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement and the Harmony Contributor Licence Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

**"Corporation"** means the legal entity named in Schedule A that signs this Corporate CLA.

**"Project Owner"** means karlmoriaty, the individual maintaining Runesgalder.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including source code, documentation, tests, design material, or other material, that the Corporation, through a Designated Contributor, intentionally submits to the Project Owner for inclusion in Runesgalder.

**"Designated Contributor"** means an individual named in Schedule B who is authorised by the Corporation to submit Contributions on its behalf.

**"Submit"** means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to the Project Owner or the project's repositories, issue trackers, discussions, or review systems, excluding communication that is clearly marked as "Not a Contribution".

**"Work"** means Runesgalder and any associated materials owned or managed by the Project Owner.

## 3. Copyright licence

Subject to the terms of this Corporate CLA, the Corporation grants the Project Owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute the Corporation's Contributions and such derivative works.

The Corporation retains copyright ownership of its Contributions. The Project Owner may make Contributions available to recipients under the MIT Licence or another OSI-approved licence.

## 4. Patent licence

Subject to the terms of this Corporate CLA, the Corporation grants the Project Owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer the Work. This licence applies only to patent claims licensable by the Corporation that are necessarily infringed by a Contribution alone or by the combination of a Contribution with the Work to which it was submitted.

If any entity institutes patent litigation against the Corporation or any other entity alleging that a Contribution, or the Work to which the Corporation contributed, constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, then any patent licence granted to that entity under this Corporate CLA for that Contribution or Work terminates as of the date the litigation is filed.

## 5. Representations

The Corporation represents that:

- The person signing this Corporate CLA is authorised to bind the Corporation to its terms.
- The Corporation is legally entitled to grant the licences in this Corporate CLA.
- Each Designated Contributor is authorised to submit Contributions on the Corporation's behalf.
- Each Contribution is the original creation of the Corporation or a Designated Contributor, unless it is a submission on behalf of another person or entity with the required permissions.
- Contribution submissions include complete details of any third-party licence or other restriction known to the Corporation that is associated with any part of the Contributions.
- The Contributions do not knowingly include material that is unlawful, malicious, or subject to a licence that conflicts with the Work's distribution terms.

The Corporation must keep Schedule B accurate and must notify the Project Owner of additions and removals of Designated Contributors.

## 6. Moral rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Corporation and its Designated Contributors waive and agree not to assert any moral rights or similar rights in the Contributions against the Project Owner, its successors, assigns, or recipients of the Work.

## 7. Designated Contributors

Only individuals listed in Schedule B may submit Contributions on the Corporation's behalf under this Corporate CLA. Schedule B records each Designated Contributor's name, GitHub username, contact email, and the date the authorisation starts or ends.

Each Designated Contributor is encouraged to sign [CLA.md](CLA.md) in their own right through CLA Assistant. Individual signature records the person; this Corporate CLA records the Corporation's rights.

## 8. No obligation

The Project Owner is not obligated to use, merge, publish, or distribute any Contribution. The Project Owner may remove a Contribution at any time.

## 9. No warranty

Contributions are provided on an "as is" basis, without warranties or conditions of any kind, whether express or implied, including warranties of title, non-infringement, merchantability, or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. Notice

The Corporation agrees to notify the Project Owner if it becomes aware of any fact or circumstance that would make any representation in this Corporate CLA inaccurate.

## 11. Term and termination

This Corporate CLA takes effect on the date it is signed and continues until terminated.

The Corporation may end authorisation for future Contributions by written notice to the Project Owner. Termination does not affect the licences granted for Contributions submitted before the termination takes effect. The Project Owner may remove any Contribution at any time under section 8.

## 12. Governing law and venue

This Corporate CLA is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules.

For the purposes of this Corporate CLA, **"Project Owner's Domicile"** means:

- (a) the Project Owner's habitual residence (经常居住地) in the People's Republic of China at the time the dispute is filed with the court; or
- (b) if the Project Owner has no such habitual residence, the Project Owner's domicile (住所地) in the People's Republic of China at that time.

The people's court with jurisdiction at the Project Owner's Domicile has exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Corporate CLA, including non-contractual disputes.

## 13. Entire agreement and amendments

This Corporate CLA, together with Schedules A and B, is the entire agreement between the Corporation and the Project Owner concerning Contributions made under it. It supersedes any prior or contemporaneous agreements on that subject. Amendments must be in writing and accepted by both parties.
